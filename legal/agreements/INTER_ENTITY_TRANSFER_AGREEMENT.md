## Agreement Terms

### 1. Irrevocable Revenue Transfer
The Transferor (Strategickhaos DAO LLC) hereby irrevocably assigns and transfers **seven percent (7%)** of all gross revenue generated from its operations to the Transferee (ValorYield Engine).

This transfer obligation is:
- **Perpetual**: Continues for the lifetime of both entities
- **Irrevocable**: Cannot be rescinded, modified, or cancelled by either party
- **Automatic**: Executed algorithmically without manual intervention
- **Priority**: Takes precedence before any other distributions or allocations

### 2. Purpose and Mission Alignment
The purpose of this transfer is to enable the Transferee to fulfill its charitable mission, including but not limited to:
- Supporting veterans through financial aid and services
- Funding medical research and patient care (St. Jude Children's Research Hospital)
- Supporting humanitarian relief efforts (Médecins Sans Frontières/Doctors Without Borders)
- Providing educational resources and financial literacy tools
- Other charitable purposes aligned with public benefit

### 3. Execution Mechanism
This agreement shall be executed through:
- **Algorithmic Implementation**: Smart contract automation via the Unified Income Distribution Protocol (UIDP)
- **No Human Override**: The transfer mechanism operates autonomously without discretionary control
- **Transparent Logging**: All transfers recorded in blockchain-backed audit logs
- **Real-time Processing**: Transfers occur immediately upon revenue recognition

### 4. Technical Implementation
The 7% transfer is implemented via:
- UIDP smart contracts located at: `/docs/contracts/uidp/`
- Automated webhook listeners for Stripe and PayPal payment processors
- NFT-based transaction receipts for audit trail
- Git-versioned logs at: `dao_treasury_log.json`

### 5. Compliance and Oversight
- All transfers comply with IRS regulations for related-party transactions
- Transfers are conducted at arm's length without preferential treatment
- Both entities maintain separate governance and decision-making authority
- Annual reporting to stakeholders via transparency reports

### 6. Beneficiaries
Initial designated beneficiaries receiving support through ValorYield Engine:
- St. Jude Children's Research Hospital
- Médecins Sans Frontières (Doctors Without Borders)
- U.S. Military Veterans (various programs)
- Educational institutions and scholarship funds

### 7. Amendment and Termination
This agreement may only be terminated or amended by:
- Unanimous vote of both entities' governance structures
- Court order or regulatory requirement
- Dissolution of either entity per applicable law

No amendment shall reduce the 7% transfer rate below the specified level.

### 8. Governing Law
This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Wyoming and applicable federal regulations.

### 9. Severability
If any provision of this agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

### 10. Entire Agreement
This document constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the subject matter herein and supersedes all prior discussions, agreements, or understandings.
